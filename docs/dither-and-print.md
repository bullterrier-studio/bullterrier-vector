# Dither, halftone and print looks

Planned 2026-11-15, not scheduled. The vector side of looks that dither and halftone tools in common use draw in pixels:
here they are paths, so they print at any size, plot with a pen and stay editable as SVG. Vector never links a raster
library: a photo arrives as a map of plain numbers (the map input of V14), and a caller joins it with bullterrier-image.
The ecosystem view is the design repository's `dither-and-print.md`.

| Item | What it is | Batch |
|---|---|---|
| Tracing a dithered label map | A palette index per pixel, in square cells of a known size, traced exactly into straight-edged regions, one group per colour, a cap on colours: dither and posterize to SVG. Curve fitting would round the cells. | V14a, built: `Drawing.trace_cells(map, cell, max_colors=)`; paint each colour with `only(level=..).fill(..)` |
| Tone-driven line families | Rows displaced by tone (ridgelines, waveforms; hidden lines removed), lines whose width carries tone along waves, rings, a spiral or rays, hatching and crosshatching by tone levels (V15b built these: stacked `hatch(below=)` steps over a `Field.map` tone, and `Field.contours` for contour lines), stippled dots. Plotter output applies. | V15c, built: `rows`, `bands` (waves, rings, spiral, rays) and `stipple`; plotter output is `to_plot_svg` (V15a and V15b built the `hatch` and the `Field.map` it rests on) |
| Halftone dot shapes | The `halftone` style draws round dots on a lattice. Add square, ellipse, diamond and a caller's path as the dot. | 4a, built: `shape=` `"dot"`, `"square"`, `"ellipse"` (with `aspect`), `"diamond"`, `"euclidean"` or a `Path`; the tone may be a `Field` |
| Halftone screen geometry | The lattice laid along waves, rings, a spiral or rays, as image's `halftone` will be: the same names and meanings, so a look reads the same in both (design.md, "Colour effects" keeps colour effects consistent the same way). | 4a, built: `screen=` `"grid"`, `"wave"`, `"rings"`, `"spiral"`, `"rays"` (image's names, meanings and ray counts; its `line` and `matrix` shapes are `bands` along the same layouts and a caller's `Path`) |
| Character grids as text | A grid of characters from a caller (image's `ascii` gives one) set as real SVG text. Needs a monospaced bundled font: the bundled fonts are proportional. Check whether `text` with lines already lays out a grid; the font may be the only gap. | 4b, built: `characters(rows, *, cell, ...)` stands one outlined glyph in each cell (outlines, not SVG `<text>`, so every output shows the same shapes; `char`, `row` and `column` stay as attributes), with image's `colors` as they come; the monospaced bundled font is JetBrains Mono |
| Overlay marks | Boxes with corner brackets, crosshairs, labels and leader lines from a caller's list of boxes and points (a detector's or a tracker's answer): the look of a heads-up display. Generators, like the repeaters. | 4b, built: `marks(boxes, points, *, bracket, cross, label_size, font)`; open strokes, a `role` and a `mark` on every shape |
